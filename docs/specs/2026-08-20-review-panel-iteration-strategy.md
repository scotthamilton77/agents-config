# Review-panel iteration strategy

**Date:** 2026-08-20 · **Status:** settled design, implementation not started
**Drives:** `agents-config-9k9.17.9` (cost-driver reassessment, PANEL-A1–A4), `agents-config-9k9.17.16` (iteration-strategy reassessment, PANEL-B1–B11), with upstream companion `agents-config-9k9.229`.
**Evidence base:** PRs #402/#403 (first two campaigns) and #556/#557 (9k9.215 campaigns, per-lens convergence data). Settled in a grilling session; the tracker items carry the observation detail.

## 1. Goals

1. **Efficient and comprehensive** detection of quality issues across work shapes, including mixed targets (e.g. a skill combining prose and scripts).
2. **No compensation for missing mechanical verification.** The panel assumes acceptance criteria were turned into mechanical tests upstream and that those tests passed. It checks for *evidence* of that and bounces targets that lack it; it never re-derives it.
3. **Minimum force, including zero.** Force is minimized against expected total cost — tokens spent plus escaped-defect interventions — not tokens alone. A zero-force verdict is legitimate only when the staffing decision can justify that no lens applies — the recorded justification (§4) is the operational test — and it is always recorded, never silent.

What is not broken survives: adversarial multi-model review stays; the settled-items ledger stays (no rebutted finding was ever re-filed) and extends to rebutted design-decision attacks.

## 2. Root causes of the four observed cost drivers (PANEL-A1)

| Cost driver (observed) | Root cause | Change class |
|---|---|---|
| Typo-class findings block at the same weight as self-contradictions (#402) | Severity is a flat mechanical/advisory binary with no class carve-out; cosmetic defects have no cheaper route than a frontier lens seat | Panel scope and lens instruction text — the profile's precondition set absorbs the mechanical pre-pass (§4) and sets force ceilings; mandates exclude cosmetic findings |
| Prose artifacts accrete under fixing; same defect class re-minted on newly written text (#402: 388→500 lines) | The fix side is entirely ungoverned — the contract instructs reviewers only, so fixes layer qualifiers onto wrong text instead of replacing it | Panel scope — the contract extends to a fix-dispatch contract (§6) |
| One lens consumed a round with unbounded output (13/20 round-1 findings from test-adequacy, #403) | Lens mandates lack stopping conditions; a lens that can always produce output always will | Lens instruction text — every mandate states what makes an instance worth reporting (PANEL-A4) |
| Termination read count while severity rose; deepest defect surfaced last (#403: 20→7→5 with rising severity) | No termination contract exists at all — no deployed round cap, no severity reading; every campaign ended by improvised human ruling | Panel scope — trend-analyst termination and the terminal sweep (§7, §5) |

## 3. Precondition gate

Emission refuses a target that lacks recorded evidence that its profile's declared mechanical gates ran green at the head SHA under review, naming the missing or failing gate. Evidence is an execution record — gate name, exit status, head SHA — never a bare assertion that the gate passed; failed, malformed, or unverifiable evidence refuses the same way as absent evidence. Evidence bound to any other head refuses as stale. The refusal verb is **bounce upstream** — the panel does not review around the gap, and it does not judge whether the upstream gates were the right ones.

"Mechanical gates" is per-profile, not one rule: an AC-derived test gate for typed code; doc-lint plus a criteria artifact whose acceptance criteria survived an ac-attack round for specs and docs; nothing at all for a profile that declares zero preconditions. A profile with an empty precondition set passes on empty evidence.

Producing this evidence is upstream's obligation, encoded as default de-facto ACs on the planning and implementation phases (`agents-config-9k9.229`): criteria red-test-convertible and ac-attack-survived before implementation; profile named at planning time; gate evidence recorded at the delivered head; scope boundaries stated in the claim. Deploy order matters: the gate is not enforced on real campaigns before that encoding lands, or every legitimate target bounces.

## 4. Classes, profiles, and staffing

**Classes stay coarse** — typed-code, spec, prose — because a class is heavy: its own lens roster, its own round per mixed target, its own verdict. A mixed target partitions by class into one staffed round and one verdict per present class, each finding attributed to exactly one class partition; a class partition whose staffing decision is a justified zero contributes its terminal staffing record in place of a verdict. Tier vocabulary is the deployed contract's: a lens declares `frontier` (hard-reasoning seats) or `mid` (mechanical-walk seats), and "frontier-tier seats" throughout this record means the lenses a class declares `frontier`. Sub-type sensitivity lives in a **profile table** the staffing step reads: one row per artifact type carrying default staffing (the lens subset staffing starts from), force ceiling (the most review force the profile permits — staffing may subtract from it but never exceed it; a zero ceiling is the zero-force default), a precondition set, and an explicit per-row no-gate marker where preconditions are waived. Minimum rows: prototype (the one no-gate minimum row — zero-force default, no preconditions), changelog (mechanical-only), agent-instruction prose (skills, rules, CLAUDE.md — mandates derived from the writing-skills discipline rather than freshly minted lens text; preconditions doc-lint plus the cosmetic pre-pass), spec (preconditions doc-lint, the cosmetic pre-pass, and the attacked criteria artifact of §3), general docs (preconditions doc-lint plus the cosmetic pre-pass). The cosmetic pre-pass is PANEL-A2's mechanical home: a spell/grammar/link checker declared as a precondition gate and run upstream like any other, so typo-class defects are caught before the panel and consume no lens seat and no round. The table is validated before use: a missing minimum row, a duplicate type, an incomplete row, or a typed-code row declaring zero preconditions refuses — the no-gate marker is the only route to an empty precondition set, so a profile cannot quietly authorize reviewing around missing tests. An unlisted type picks a listed profile explicitly and records the choice and the reason — never an improvised lens set; a target whose profile cannot be resolved at all refuses as a resolution error, distinct from a no-gate pass. A new sub-type is a row edit, not a design event. Splitting the panel into per-shape skills instead moves classification upstream to an invoker with less information, multiplies deployed surface, and duplicates the round/verdict machinery that must stay identical — the mixed-target case (one round per class over a partitioned target) is the argument for one skill, and it is already the incumbent architecture.

**Staffing is a first-class, per-round recorded artifact**: a foreign model — one from outside the reviewing session's own vendor family, for independence — at the routing table's mid tier recommends which of the class's lenses actually apply to this target. The record names the recommending model, and carries a rationale per *excluded* roster lens — a blanket "not needed" cannot silently drop the security seat from a target full of authentication logic. Interactive sessions present the recommendation to the user, and the user's final edit is what gets recorded as the decision; non-interactive runs record the recommendation and proceed. Staffing is **subtract-only** — the class roster is the ceiling. The verdict references the staffing record, and verdict completeness is validated against it rather than the class table, so a silently dropped seat still fails validation. A zero-lens decision with non-blank justification is itself the terminal record and no verdict exists for it; a zero-lens outcome arising from a missing or failed recommendation refuses instead — an empty seat map is never mistaken for a considered zero.

**Embedded prose** (comments, docstrings — the most persistent defect class on #557, reviewed like code while rotting like prose) is a lens duty, not a fourth class: the typed-code documentation-quality mandate explicitly owns narration, drift, and history-vs-decision rot, with a stopping condition under which each finding names the misreading the comment causes. A merely brief comment is not a finding. The fix-side narration clause (§6) attacks the same class from the other flank. That clause is also the general form PANEL-A4 demands: a stopping condition is a mandate clause naming the observable each finding must cite — the misreading caused, for embedded prose; the failure the missing test would have caught, for test-adequacy — and a mandate lacking such a clause fails content review at implementation.

## 5. Round lifecycle

1. **Round 1 — whole artifact**, staffed per the staffing decision. A clean, complete full round 1 is terminal directly: it is already a whole-artifact clean read by a superset of the sweep seats.
2. **Rounds ≥2 — delta-scoped by default.** Each surviving lens reads only the change since the head it last judged, plus the disposition ledger. Staffing is re-evaluated every round from the finding trend and the nature of the fixes; lenses that went clean drop, and a lens whose delta is empty — nothing changed since the head it judged — is not staffed at all, which is cheaper than an empty dispatch. A newly re-staffed lens (no prior head) reads full. The **accretion trigger** forces a full rescope: fixes since the last full read have grown beyond trivial (the same implementation-defined, edge-tested boundary as §6 clause 3), or a finding shows an original assumption was wrong.
3. **Terminal sweep — exactly one whole-artifact pass** before terminal-clean is declared on any campaign that reached its zero-blocking round through deltas. Unconditional by contract, not triggered by a detector: the exit door has one full re-read built into the frame, because the late finds on both evidence campaigns (#403 round 3, #557 round 6 on code unchanged since round 1) are invisible to delta scoping by construction. Staffed by the class's frontier-tier seats regardless of what earlier staffing subtracted, framed blocking-only ("confirm no blocking defect exists" — a verdict, not a findings hunt), ledger loaded. A class with no frontier-tier seat to staff escalates to the human rather than declaring terminal-clean — a sweep nobody flew is not a sweep. Sweep findings re-enter the normal fix → delta-verify loop; no second sweep follows unless the accretion trigger re-arms it.

Making per-round full re-reads the default instead is what produced the churn: constant re-litigation surface, and exhausted seats turning into findings-generators (#557's security seat sat silent four rounds, then burst with rebutted design attacks).

## 6. Fix-dispatch contract

A findings round emits a fix dispatch alongside its verdict; a clean round emits none. The dispatch references each blocking finding it addresses — "clean up the affected section" maps to nothing and validates as incomplete. The review contract and the fix contract are one loop with one economics — the evidence shows the fixer driving the accretion spiral (three of four narration-class recurrences on #557 were written *during* fix passes), and the one lens whose findings decayed to zero and stayed there was the one whose fixes carried mutation evidence. Four clauses:

1. **Smallest net change** that achieves the fix — no over-explaining, no unnecessary code, no pointless prose.
2. **Mutation evidence for code fixes** — the test fails without the fix. In the ledger, `fixed` on a typed-code mechanical finding requires evidence naming the test and the fails-without/passes-with observation, exactly as `rebutted` already requires evidence; a bare "fixed" claim is as inadmissible as a bare rebuttal.
3. **Replacement-first for prose** — prefer replacing incorrect text over adding qualifying text; a fix that grows the artifact states why replacement couldn't achieve it. Non-trivial net growth in a fix round re-staffs the consistency lens next round, diff-scoped over the new text: growth is paid for with reading, not forbidden. The triviality boundary is defined by the implementation and tested at its edges — zero, negative, and boundary-sized net growth each produce a documented rescoping decision. A hard no-net-growth rule forbids the correct fix for any gap finding and invites clarity-destroying compression; a numeric growth budget has the wrong shape — one legitimate section busts it while five scattered qualifiers sneak under it.
4. **Narration sweep** — no transition commentary minted under review pressure ("as before", "has ever claimed"); prose states the current decision, not the fix's history.

## 7. Dispositions, blame, and termination

**Blame split at round assembly.** The assembler (not a new seat — the split is git-blame-assisted and the assembler already touches every finding) attributes each finding: PR-induced or missed-in-scope stays in the campaign; pre-existing and non-blocking transfers out. The disposition set gains **`transferred`**, valid only with a pre-existing blame attribution whose evidence includes the provenance basis — a base-side reference showing the defect predates the change, not just the assembler's say-so — plus a filed work-item id, carried to re-reviews through the settled-items channel as out-of-scope-accounted. A pre-existing *blocking* finding is not transferable. Blame truth itself is judgment; the recorded attribution and its provenance basis are what stay mechanical and auditable.

**The bent ruler halts.** A finding that indicts the acceptance criteria or requirements themselves — as opposed to pre-existing artifact content — produces a halted verdict with an upstream-defect halt reason, never another round: every further round would measure against a ruler known to be bent. The campaign resumes only after the indicted upstream artifact has actually changed; a resume request over an unchanged ruler refuses. This is the concrete mechanism for "re-review in aggregate when original assumptions were incorrect."

**Fixer decisions are accepted within rounds.** No lens re-litigates a settled item; rebutted design-decision attacks enter the settled ledger like any rebuttal, and a finding matching a settled-ledger item does not re-enter the campaign at round assembly. Blocking-severity rebuttals accumulate into the terminal verdict for the human ruling.

**Termination has no numeric round cap.** A count cap reads the wrong signal — the observed campaigns fell 20→7→5 in count while severity rose. Termination is: terminal-clean (§5), or a **trend-analyst verdict**. A checkpoint fires after every second consecutive non-clean round since the last checkpoint, the first after round 2; a zero-blocking round exits to the terminal sweep instead, so no checkpoint ever follows a clean round. At each checkpoint a Fable-high trend-analysis dispatch reads the campaign — finding trends per lens, fix history, severity direction — and answers whether the findings indicate an upstream problem (missing/ambiguous ACs, design flaw) or a lens that is bikeshedding. Its recorded verdict is one of: **(a) continue two more rounds**, carrying staffing advice (a bikeshedding lens is de-staffed); **(b) terminate and bounce upstream** for remediation; **(c) terminate and escalate to the human**. The verdict cites the campaign evidence supporting it — the per-round trends, severity direction, and fix history it read — and an uncited verdict is invalid, resolving as (c). Severity rising while count falls forbids verdict (a): the analyst returns (b) or (c), never continuation. The checkpoint record is retained as a first-class campaign record alongside the staffing decisions; consumption is observable, because the next staffing record cites the checkpoint verdict and reflects its advice. The record also carries its origin — a returned verdict, or a dispatch failure — so a failure-escalation is distinguishable from an analyst-chosen one. Failure of the analyst dispatch or of its required inputs — retained verdicts missing, trend data malformed, the model unavailable — resolves as (c): the machine fails toward the human, never toward silent continuation. The analyst is the covert-case complement to the bent-ruler halt, which fires in-round when a single finding overtly indicts the upstream artifact. This is a standing Fable-high spend, bounded to campaigns that have already burned two rounds without cleaning; it is the deliberate top-tier-for-judgment seat, and this record — adopted by the owner — is the standing authorization the deployed delegation rule requires before a Fable spawn: the skill's dispatch cites it, and if that authorization is ever withdrawn the checkpoint resolves as (c).

## 8. Acceptance criteria

PANEL-A1–A4 stand on `agents-config-9k9.17.9` (A1 is satisfied by §2; A3's termination rule is the §7 analyst — one mechanism, not two, and its severity clause is pinned inside PANEL-B7). PANEL-B1–B11 are recorded as acceptance on `agents-config-9k9.17.16` via `work acceptance set`; this section is the same contract in full.

- **PANEL-B1** (§3): Emission refuses a target lacking recorded evidence that its profile's declared mechanical gates ran green at the reviewed head SHA, naming the missing or failing gate. Evidence is an execution record — gate name, exit status, head SHA — never a bare assertion; failed, malformed, or unverifiable evidence refuses like absent evidence, and evidence bound to any other head refuses as stale. A no-gate profile passes on empty evidence.
- **PANEL-B2** (§4): Each round's emission consumes a recorded per-round staffing decision: the lens subset (subtract-only from the class roster), the recommending model, a rationale per excluded roster lens, and — interactively — the user's final edit as the recorded decision. The verdict references it; the validator checks lens coverage against the staffing record, not the class table; a staffed lens outside the roster fails validation. A zero-lens decision with non-blank justification is itself the terminal record with no verdict; a zero-lens outcome from a missing or failed recommendation refuses instead.
- **PANEL-B3** (§5): Round ≥2 prompts scope every surviving lens to the delta since the head that lens last judged, plus the disposition ledger; round 1 and newly staffed lenses read full; a lens with an empty delta is not staffed that round. The accretion conditions — growth since the last full read beyond the same implementation-defined, edge-tested triviality boundary as PANEL-B5's, or a finding invalidating an original assumption — force a full rescope.
- **PANEL-B4** (§5): Terminal-clean reached through delta rounds requires exactly one whole-artifact sweep — the class's frontier-tier seats regardless of earlier subtraction, blocking-only framing, ledger loaded — after a zero-blocking delta round; a class with no frontier-tier seat escalates to the human instead of declaring terminal-clean. Sweep findings re-enter the fix/delta-verify loop with no second sweep unless the accretion trigger re-arms it. A clean, complete full round 1 is terminal directly.
- **PANEL-B5** (§6): A findings round emits a fix dispatch referencing each blocking finding it addresses and carrying the four clauses: smallest net change; mutation evidence for code fixes; replacement-first for prose, with non-trivial growth (a boundary the implementation defines and tests at zero, negative, and edge values) justified and re-staffing a diff-scoped consistency read next round; the narration sweep. A clean round emits no dispatch. The ledger validator requires evidence on `fixed` for typed-code mechanical findings naming the test and the fails-without/passes-with observation, as `rebutted` already requires evidence.
- **PANEL-B6** (§7): The disposition set gains `transferred`, valid only with a pre-existing blame attribution whose evidence includes the provenance basis (a base-side reference showing the defect predates the change) plus a filed work-item id, carried to re-reviews via the settled-items channel; a pre-existing blocking finding is not transferable. A finding indicting the ACs/requirements yields a halted verdict with an upstream-defect halt reason, never another round; the campaign resumes only after the indicted upstream artifact has changed.
- **PANEL-B7** (§7): A trend-analysis checkpoint fires after every second consecutive non-clean round since the last checkpoint, the first after round 2, and never after a clean round. Each checkpoint is a Fable-high dispatch whose recorded verdict is one of continue-2-with-staffing-advice / terminate-bounce-upstream / terminate-escalate-human and cites the campaign evidence supporting it; severity rising while count falls forbids the continue verdict. The checkpoint record is retained as a first-class campaign record carrying its origin (returned verdict or dispatch failure), and the next staffing record cites it and reflects its advice. Failure of the dispatch or of its required inputs, and an uncited verdict, resolve as escalate-to-human; no numeric round cap exists.
- **PANEL-B8** (§4): The triage contract carries the artifact-type profile table (default staffing, force ceiling, precondition set, and a per-row no-gate marker) covering at least prototype (the no-gate row), changelog (mechanical-only), agent-instruction prose (mandates derived from the writing-skills discipline), spec, and general docs. Table validation refuses a missing minimum row, a duplicate type, an incomplete row, or a typed-code row declaring zero preconditions — the no-gate marker is the only route to an empty precondition set. An unlisted type picks a listed profile explicitly and records the choice and the reason; an unresolvable profile refuses as a resolution error, distinct from a no-gate pass.
- **PANEL-B9** (§4): The typed-code documentation-quality mandate explicitly owns embedded-prose rot with a stopping condition under which each finding names the misreading the comment causes — a contradiction with current behaviour or decision qualifies without an observed victim; a merely brief comment is not a finding.
- **PANEL-B10** (§4): A mixed target partitions by class into one staffed round and one verdict per present class, each finding attributed to exactly one class partition; a class partition with a justified zero-lens staffing decision contributes that terminal record in place of a verdict.
- **PANEL-B11** (§7): A finding matching a settled-ledger item — including a rebutted design-decision attack — does not re-enter the campaign at round assembly; each suppression records the settled item id it matched, so the filter is auditable, and an exact re-citation of a settled id suppresses mechanically.

## 9. Implementation notes and deliberate deferrals

- **The revision changes the verdict envelope** (shared `review-verdict` skill — wider blast radius than the Claude-only panel): it adds a staffing-record reference, the `transferred` disposition, the upstream-defect halt reason, and required evidence on `fixed` for typed-code mechanical findings, and completeness condition 4 now reads the staffing record instead of the class table. The envelope stores no per-lens counts; the trend analyst reads across retained per-round verdicts rather than requiring a trend field in the schema. The staffing decision and the trend-analysis checkpoint are new first-class retained records beside the verdicts, not envelope fields.
- **Three terminal shapes exist**, and the merge-eligibility evaluator reads each: a terminal-clean verdict (eligible); a halted verdict with the upstream-defect reason (not eligible until the indicted artifact changes and the campaign resumes); and the non-verdict terminals — an analyst termination record, or a justified zero-lens staffing record, which is the terminal artifact merge eligibility reads when the profile's preconditions passed.
- **Lens text is gated code**, not prose files: mandates live in `src/user/.claude/skills/review-panel/contracts.json` and boilerplate in `src/user/.claude/skills/review-panel/emit_prompts.py`, both under paired test suites. `src/user/.claude/skills/review-panel/SKILL.md` sits at 1984/2000 tokens against the hard per-skill cap — measure on current main before budgeting any prose there; substance lands in the contracts file and scripts.
- **No size gate.** An oversized multi-concern PR is an upstream failure; partition machinery inside the review would compensate for it, and no volume-induced panel failure has been observed. Deferred until one is — that observation is the admission record for a preflight bound whose verb is the same bounce as §3.
- **Interactive grilling surface** for staffing float-by and session UX is separate work (`agents-config-9k9.221`).
