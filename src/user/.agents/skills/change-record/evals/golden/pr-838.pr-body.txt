## Changes
- **Routing.** A human answer with a note or free text always goes to the expert.
- **Effort.** A typed answer that opens nothing runs at the task effort. It keeps the heavy effort on a channel the human moved to the expert.
- **Spec text.** Three spec passages now say the expert weighs the note.
<details><summary>Design notes</summary>

The expert picks the effort by one rule. A turn that carries an impact task runs at the task effort on every channel, including one the human moved to the expert. A typed turn with no task runs at the task effort on a channel the human has not moved. The same turn runs at the heavy effort on a channel the human moved to the expert themselves, because they chose that effort for the channel. A channel the escalation policy moved counts as not moved by the human.

The expert driver tells a human move from a policy move off the log, at the moment it picks the effort. It uses the two readings the escalation module already provides. When nothing is downstream of a typed answer, no impact task is recorded and nothing waits.

The map's first seat never reads the human's prose from this change on, because every map answer carrying text goes straight to the expert. The map's prose escalation conditions are therefore unreachable there, and threads keep them. Their code and the page's map-toggle highlight stay, because pruning them is separate work.

The three amended passages sit in two specs, and each now cites PND-D4 (the spec decision that seats every typed answer on the expert). They are GUI-A33 (the criterion on highlighting a channel's way to the expert), GUI-D48 (the decision on which channels the escalation conditions reach) and that decision's third trigger.

Six existing tests used a typed map answer only as a vehicle, and they now pick a plain option. Five tests of the map's prose conditions now run on a thread. One browser test moved to a thread and was renamed, and its evidence row was re-pointed. In the evidence ledgers, PND-A2 (the criterion on seating a typed answer) goes from open to twelve test rows. PND-A11 (the criterion on the backpressure paragraph) goes from open to four.
